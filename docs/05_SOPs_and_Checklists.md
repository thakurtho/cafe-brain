# Musafir Cafe — SOPs & Checklists (Draft, Dummy Data)

Owner: Ritika Bisht (Outlet Manager), unless noted otherwise. All drafts — meant for testing the capture/approval flow, not as final operating standards.

## Opening Checklist
1. Unlock and switch on all equipment (espresso machine, grinder, POS)
2. Machine warm-up — 15 minutes minimum before first shot (photo proof)
3. Grinder calibration check — pull a test shot, confirm 25–30 second extraction (reading: extraction time in seconds)
4. Milk stock check — confirm sufficient stock for the shift (voice confirm)
5. Wipe down counters, restock napkins/sugar/stirrers
6. Review handover notes from previous shift, acknowledge or flag discrepancy
7. Cash float count (reading: ₹ amount)

## Closing Checklist
1. Machine backflush and clean (photo proof, per Machine Cleaning SOP below)
2. Grinder hopper emptied and wiped if closing for the night
3. Till count and reconciliation (reading: ₹ amount)
4. Wastage log submitted (via Tell — voice or photo)
5. Fridge and dairy stock check, note anything nearing expiry
6. Floor swept, chairs stacked, trash taken out
7. All equipment powered down except fridge

## Machine Cleaning SOP (linked: Benki Bombat 2GR)
1. Backflush with blind filter and cleaning detergent — daily, end of day
2. Wipe steam wand after every use, purge before and after steaming
3. Group head cleaned with brush — daily
4. Full descale — every 4–6 weeks depending on water hardness (compliance-style reminder, not a daily task)

## Milk Steaming SOP
1. Cold milk only, fill pitcher to just below the spout line
2. Purge steam wand before inserting
3. Position wand just below milk surface for aeration (5–8 seconds), then submerge for heating
4. Target 60–65°C (do not exceed 70°C — scalding affects taste)
5. Swirl to integrate foam before pouring

## Order-Taking SOP
1. Greet customer, confirm dine-in or takeaway
2. Confirm any customisation (sugar level, milk type) at time of order
3. Repeat order back before billing
4. For repeat customers, check customer notes if available (e.g. "usual order")

## Spill / Breakage SOP
1. Cordon off the area if there's a slip hazard
2. Clean up immediately
3. Log via Tell (voice + photo) — becomes an Observation unless injury or ongoing hazard, in which case it's an Incident requiring resolution before shift close

## Customer Complaint Handling SOP
1. Listen without interrupting, acknowledge the issue
2. Offer to remake the drink or adjust the bill, at staff discretion for minor issues
3. For anything beyond a simple remake, escalate to Outlet Manager during the shift
4. Log the interaction via Tell — this feeds the judgment-call capture mechanism if a discretionary call was made
