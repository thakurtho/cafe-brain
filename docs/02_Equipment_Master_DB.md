# Equipment Master DB — Commercial Coffee Machines, Grinders & Roasters (India)

Proprietary reference compiled from India's main commercial coffee equipment sellers (Benki Brewing Tools, Kaapi Solutions, Kaapi Machines, Brewing Gadgets). Intended as a standing lookup table the Brain can match an outlet's registered machine against — for SOP linkage, troubleshooting guidance, and service-interval defaults. Most B2B listings in this market are enquiry-priced rather than listed; price bands below are broad indicative ranges for planning, not quotes.

## Suggested SOP linkage
Each registered machine/grinder should link to:
- A cleaning & descaling SOP (frequency depends on water hardness and daily volume)
- A calibration checklist item (grind size, dose)
- A manufacturer troubleshooting reference for the "what do I do now" assist flow when a machine fault is reported
